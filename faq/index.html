FAQ

Clear answers before the first call.

General information about appointments, language assistance, and the consultation-request process.

How much is a consultation?

A consultation is $150 for up to 30 minutes. If additional time is needed, the attorney will discuss the applicable charge with you.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Virtual consultations are available when appropriate. Contact the office to discuss scheduling.

Can I request Spanish or Haitian Creole language assistance?

Yes. Spanish and Haitian Creole language assistance is available through the firm’s staff as needed. Let the office know when requesting a consultation.

Do you serve clients outside Sebring?

Yes. The firm has offices in Sebring and Wauchula and welcomes clients from Arcadia, elsewhere in Florida, and out of state when appropriate.

What should I include in a website request?

Please provide only basic scheduling information, such as your name, contact information, and general practice area. Do not send confidential case details or documents through the website.

Does submitting a website request create an attorney-client relationship?

No. Sending a request does not create an attorney-client relationship. The office will follow up about scheduling a consultation.
