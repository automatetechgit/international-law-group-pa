Website privacy notice

This concept-site notice explains how the current consultation-request helper works.

Consultation requests

The consultation-request helper prepares an email addressed to International Law Group PA. It is not a secure client-intake portal. Do not submit confidential facts, documents, financial information, or sensitive personal information through the website.

Attorney-client relationship

Contacting the firm through the website does not create an attorney-client relationship. Representation begins only after the firm has agreed to represent you under an appropriate agreement.

Website measurement

The launch plan currently calls for minimal site measurement. This notice will be updated before the production launch if analytics or other tools are added.
